COASTAL CONSTRUCTION FASTENER STANDARD
Issued by the industry fastener body
Effective: 2022-06-01

Scope
  Applies to exterior construction within one mile of a saltwater shoreline.

Fastener requirements
  Stainless steel fasteners are required for any deck near salt air.
  Hot-dip galvanized fasteners are not sufficient in a coastal exposure zone.
  Hidden fastener systems must use stainless components throughout.

Rationale
  Salt air accelerates corrosion of plated and galvanized fasteners, which can
  compromise structural connections well before the end of a deck's service life.
