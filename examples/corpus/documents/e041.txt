COUNTY PERMIT OFFICE
Deck & Electrical Guidance
Effective: 2024-03-01

Deck permit applications
  Submit a site plan and a joist layout with the application.
  A building permit is required for any deck more than 30 inches above grade.
  Decks over 200 square feet also require a structural plan review.
  Typical residential deck permit review takes about two weeks.

Electrical permits
  An electrical permit is required before adding any new branch circuit.
  Label the panel schedule before the rough-in inspection.

Contact the permit office during business hours with questions.
