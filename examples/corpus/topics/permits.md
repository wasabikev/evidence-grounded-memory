# Permits

## Deck permits
- A building permit is required for any deck more than 30 inches above grade. [doc:e037·A]
- Decks over 200 square feet also require a structural plan review. [doc:e037·A]
- The permit office advises submitting a site plan and joist layout with the application. [doc:e041·B]
- I was told a typical residential deck permit review takes about two weeks. [user]

## Electrical permits
- An electrical permit is required before adding any new branch circuit. [doc:e037·A]
- Permit code R105.2 lists the minor repairs that are exempt from a permit. [doc:e037·A]
- The inspector wants the panel schedule labeled before the rough-in inspection. [doc:e041·B]
